<task>
Classify the medical condition term into exactly one category.
</task>

<categories>
Clinical: diseases, injuries, symptoms and signs (even when tagged "(finding)"),
pregnancy complications, suspected conditions, mental-health findings, obesity or BMI,
and diagnosed disorders of any kind, including substance-use disorders and overdose.
Physiological: normal physiological states that are medically managed but are not
disease, such as normal pregnancy.
Social: employment, education, housing, transport, criminal record, military service,
refugee status, social isolation, abuse or violence, and lifestyle behaviours or risk
factors (smoking, drug misuse, alcohol drinking, risk activities) even when tagged
"(finding)".
Historical record: past events or past conditions recorded as "History of ...".
These are Historical record even when the original event was a serious disease.
</categories>

<rules>
- Ignore bracket tags such as (finding), (situation) or (disorder) when deciding.
- A term that starts with "History of" is Historical record, not Clinical.
- A symptom is Clinical even when it is tagged as a finding.
- A behaviour or risk factor is Social. A diagnosed disorder or its acute consequence is Clinical.
- If you cannot decide, answer "Unclear" instead of guessing.
</rules>
